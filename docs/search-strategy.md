# Global Search Strategy

Search is global by default. No region is excluded a priori — a country/region earns priority dynamically based on signal, not an assumption.

## Target Region Signals (re-score periodically, don't hardcode a fixed list)
Favor regions where:
- Revit / BIM / architecture demand is currently strong (job board volume, recent postings).
- International applicants can realistically apply (no local-license-only hard requirement at this experience level).
- Visa sponsorship is offered or common in the sector.
- Remote work is genuinely possible for the role.
- Experience level (4+ years, pre-licensure) matches typical seniority expectations.
- Salary vs. relocation cost is attractive.

Regions to actively scan each cycle: Middle East & Gulf, Europe, UK, North America (US/Canada),
Australia & New Zealand, Asia, Africa, and Remote/Worldwide listings. Log findings in
`tracking/companies.csv` / `tracking/jobs.csv` with the country as data, not as a filter.

## Sources (beyond job boards)
1. Job boards (LinkedIn Jobs, Indeed, Bayt, GulfTalent, Naukrigulf, Glassdoor, ArchDaily jobs, etc.)
2. Company career pages (direct)
3. Architecture firms / engineering consultancies
4. BIM consultancies
5. Contractors / design-build firms
6. Real estate developers
7. Interior design studios
8. Visualization studios
9. Landscape companies
10. Freelance platforms (Upwork, Fiverr, specialized Revit/BIM freelance boards)
11. Professional communities (BIM forums, Revit forums, Reddit r/Revit, r/architecture)
12. Recent company announcements (new offices, project wins, expansions — signals future hiring)
13. Recruitment firms specializing in AEC
14. LinkedIn posts from architecture/BIM professionals and recruiters

## Review Cadence
Re-evaluate this strategy monthly (or after every ~20 logged opportunities): which titles,
countries, and sources actually produced interviews/responses in `tracking/applications.csv`,
and bias future searching toward those.
