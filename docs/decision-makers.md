# Decision-Maker Discovery & Prioritization

For each high-priority company (score ≥60 or strategically interesting even without a vacancy),
identify people in these roles:

Talent Acquisition, Recruiter, HR Manager, Recruitment Manager, BIM Manager, BIM Director,
BIM Lead, Technical Director, Design Director, Architecture Director, Project Director,
Managing Director, Founder, Senior Architect, Department Head.

## Priority Categories

- **A — Hiring / decision-making authority**: can directly initiate or approve a hire
  (HR/TA, hiring managers, BIM/Design Directors, Founders/MDs at small firms).
- **B — Strong professional connection**: works closely with the role (senior architects,
  department heads, project leads) — valuable for referral or inside information.
- **C — Industry network / future opportunity**: broader professional network, not an
  immediate hiring lever, but useful for visibility and long-term relationship building.

## Workflow

1. Log the contact in `tracking/contacts.csv` with company, role, category, and source/evidence.
2. Draft outreach using `templates/outreach-message.md` — personalized, referencing the company's
   actual projects or BIM activity, never generic.
3. **All LinkedIn connection requests and messages are reviewed and sent by Abdelrhman personally.**
   This system drafts; it does not auto-send. No automated LinkedIn actions.
4. Track response/outcome in `tracking/contacts.csv` (status column) to learn which messaging
   angles convert, and feed that back into `templates/outreach-message.md`.
